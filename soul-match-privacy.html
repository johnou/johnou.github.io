Overview

Soul Match does not require an account and does not ask for personal information. The names you enter are used locally on your device to generate the love score and message. We do not collect or store those names.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Soul Match uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve, measure, and improve ads. This data is handled by Google according to their privacy policies.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Data Handling Practices

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data. If our data practices change, we will update this policy.

Data Security and Retention

We do not retain personal data. Ad-related data collected by AdMob is retained and processed by Google under their policies.
